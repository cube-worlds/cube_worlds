How it works

From avatar to artifact in four steps

📸

1 · Pick a face

Choose one of your Telegram profile photos or upload any picture you like. It becomes the soul of your pass.

⚒️

2 · Forge

Each generation try burns $CUBE and produces a unique pixel-art rendering. Not feeling it? Forge again until it's you.

📜

3 · Submit

Bind your TON wallet and submit the draft you love. Every pass is reviewed by a human — the collection stays curated.

💎

4 · Own it

Approved passes are minted as NFTs straight to your wallet on TON, joining the Cube Worlds collection on Getgems.

$CUBE

Fuel for the forge

$CUBE is the in-app currency (database-only, not a token). Earn it with the daily claim streak, by inviting friends, by donating TON from your bound wallet, or top it up with Telegram Stars — then spend it on forging tries.
