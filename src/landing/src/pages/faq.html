Questions

Good to know

Where do I actually do this?

Entirely inside Telegram. This site is an information hub — the app runs as a Mini App in @cube_worlds_bot. There's nothing playable on this website by design.

What is the pass?

A one-of-a-kind pixel-art NFT forged from your own avatar and minted to your TON wallet. It's your identity in the collection today and your key to World I when the game opens — pass holders enter first.

How does forging work?

Pick a Telegram profile photo or upload an image, then generate. Each try costs $CUBE and produces a new pixel-art rendering — forge as many times as you like. When one feels right, bind your wallet and submit it for review.

Why is there a review?

Every submission is approved or declined by a human. That keeps the collection curated — no spam, no junk floods. If your draft is declined you can forge a new look and resubmit (spent tries stay spent).

Is $CUBE a tradable token?

No. $CUBE is a database-only in-app currency. You earn it from the daily claim streak, referrals, and TON donations, or top it up with Telegram Stars — and you spend it on forging tries. There is no on-chain jetton and no token listing.

What chain is this on?

The Open Network (TON). NFT minting, wallet binding (ton_proof), and donations are on-chain; everything else is app state. The collection lives on Getgems.

When does the game open?

World I is in development. The pass comes first — holders get in the moment the gates open. Watch @cube_worlds for the signal.
