# v3 — The Pass Forge

> 🚧 **In progress.** v3 is under active development and replaces v2, which was built end-to-end but never shipped (production stayed on v1 the whole time).

## The reset

v2 grew into a full ARPG — castles, heroes, dungeons, a weekly boss, PvP, tournaments, a USDT money rail. Impressive on paper, but it never felt clear. Too many systems, no single moment that mattered.

v3 cuts back to the one moment that always worked: **forging your NFT**. Everything else was deleted (it lives on in git history) and the product became one sharp loop.

## The loop

1. **Pick a face** — one of your Telegram profile photos, or upload any image.
2. **Forge** — every generation try costs $CUBE and produces a unique pixel-art rendering. Keep forging until it's you.
3. **Submit** — bind your TON wallet and submit the draft you love. A human approves or declines every pass; the collection stays curated.
4. **Own** — approved passes are minted on TON to your wallet, into the existing [Getgems collection](https://getgems.io/cubeworlds).

The v2 "escalating mint floor" queue is gone — paying per try is both the creative loop and the anti-spam. $CUBE comes from the daily claim streak, referrals, TON donations, and (new in v3) Telegram Stars top-ups.

## The stack change

The Vue 3 webview was deleted with the game sprawl. v3 is **React** (Vite, TON Connect), with a new pixel design language — Press Start 2P, VT323, dark purple and gold, hard edges. Less dashboard, more artifact.

## What comes after

The pass is the key. **World I** — the game behind the gate — is designed next, and pass holders enter first.
